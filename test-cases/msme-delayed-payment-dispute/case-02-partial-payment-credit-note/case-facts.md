# Case 02 — Partial payments, credit note and unsupported penalty

**Case ID:** SYNTH-MSME-002  
**Snapshot date:** 2026-10-18  
**Parties:** Cedar Works Engineering (fictional supplier) vs. Meridian Facilities Ltd. (fictional buyer)

Cedar supplied fabricated support frames under PO `SYNTH-PO-260701B`; the receiving record accepts all 400 units. Invoice `SYNTH-INV-260701B` totals ₹472,000 including 18% GST. Cedar later issued credit note `SYNTH-CN-260815` for ₹47,200 including GST, tied to a mutually agreed price reduction for a changed finish specification. Buyer made confirmed payments of ₹150,000 and ₹100,000. The buyer additionally claims a ₹30,000 “late installation penalty,” but the supplied PO has no such clause and there is no signed debit note or evidence that Cedar agreed to it.

Written terms say payment within 30 calendar days of acceptance. The acceptance date is 1 July 2026; supplier's ledger records a due date of 31 July 2026.
