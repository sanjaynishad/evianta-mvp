# Case 04 — Invoice issued, but delivery/acceptance proof is missing

**Case ID:** SYNTH-MSME-004  
**Snapshot date:** 2026-10-18  
**Parties:** Summit Cable Assemblies (fictional supplier) vs. Orion Controls India Ltd. (fictional buyer)

Summit issued an invoice for 60 cable harness kits for ₹180,000 plus ₹32,400 GST, total ₹212,400. The PO states payment is due 30 calendar days after acceptance. Summit has a dispatch challan and a carrier tracking page with a scan saying “arrived at destination hub”; no signed proof of delivery (POD), buyer gate entry, goods receipt note or receiving signature is included. The buyer emailed two days later stating that its plant had not received the consignment and requested POD. The packet does not resolve whether the goods were delivered to the buyer or accepted/deemed accepted.

The case is designed to test whether an agent recognizes that dispatch/invoice evidence does not establish delivery by itself.
