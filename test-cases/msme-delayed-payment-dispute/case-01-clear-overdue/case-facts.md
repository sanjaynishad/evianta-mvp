# Case 01 — Clear overdue invoice for accepted goods

**Case ID:** SYNTH-MSME-001  
**Snapshot date:** 2026-10-18  
**Parties:** BluePeak Precision Components (fictional supplier) vs. Northstar Appliances Pvt. Ltd. (fictional buyer)

BluePeak supplied 200 precision brackets under PO `SYNTH-PO-260701`. The buyer's authorized receiving record confirms all 200 units were received and accepted on 15 July 2026 with no shortage or quality note. Invoice `SYNTH-INV-260715` was issued the same day for ₹200,000 before GST plus ₹36,000 GST (18%), total ₹236,000. Written terms state payment within 30 calendar days of acceptance. The buyer has made no payment and has not raised a documented dispute. In an email, the buyer says the invoice is approved but delayed due to cash-flow constraints.

The supplier's synthetic registration record says it was classified as a **Micro** enterprise and registered before the order/supply. This is designed as the clean baseline case; still verify eligibility details in any real matter.
