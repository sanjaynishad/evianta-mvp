# Case 06 — Multiple overdue invoices and conflicting payment allocation

**Case ID:** SYNTH-MSME-006  
**Snapshot date:** 2026-10-18  
**Parties:** Riverbend Electrical Assemblies (fictional supplier) vs. Apex Process Automation Ltd. (fictional buyer)

Two invoices are outstanding for accepted supplies. Invoice A totals ₹118,000 and Invoice B totals ₹59,000. Two payments totalling ₹75,000 are evidenced. The supplier ledger allocates both payments to Invoice A; the buyer AP export allocates the ₹50,000 payment to Invoice B and the ₹25,000 payment to Invoice A. Neither payment has a remittance advice or matching reference specifying the invoice allocation. The buyer also asserts a ₹25,000 service-level penalty, but the supplied PO has no penalty clause and the buyer has provided no accepted debit note. The aggregate principal is calculable, but per-invoice balances, overdue days and any interest are allocation-sensitive.
