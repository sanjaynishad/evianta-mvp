# Case 03 — Partial rejection and promised credit note not issued

**Case ID:** SYNTH-MSME-003  
**Snapshot date:** 2026-10-18  
**Parties:** Delta Packaging Systems (fictional supplier) vs. Kestrel Foods Manufacturing Ltd. (fictional buyer)

Delta invoiced 100 sealing units at ₹3,000 each before GST. The invoice totals ₹354,000 including 18% GST. The buyer's quality report, made the day after delivery, records 80 units accepted and 20 rejected because seals failed the agreed test. The supplier's email acknowledges the reported failure and offers either replacement of the 20 rejected units or a credit note equal to 20% of the invoice; the buyer requests a credit note. No credit note or replacement evidence is present in the packet. Buyer paid ₹283,200, exactly 80% of the gross invoice, and withholds the remaining ₹70,800 pending resolution.

The packet is deliberately a contested case, not a clean late-payment example.
